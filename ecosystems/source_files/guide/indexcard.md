## Index Card Model (3" × 5", handwritten, front & back) {#card}

Your card must be **handwritten**, with your **first & last name and period** visible. Copy what *you* need. Writing small, using abbreviations and arrows, and choosing what to include is itself great studying. Below is a model of what fits.

<div class="idx-card" markdown="1">
<div class="idx-title">FRONT · Name ______ Period ___</div>

**SPHERES** Atmo = air/weather/climate · Hydro = ALL water (ice, glaciers, snow, soil moisture, aquifers) · Bio = life (coral reefs, bacteria) · Geo = rock, soil minerals, volcanoes, fossils, magnetism · Sun drives most; core drives geo · Closed system: energy in/out, matter recycled

**RELATIONSHIPS** Mutualism +/+ (bee–flower, lichen) · Commensalism +/0 (barnacle–whale) · Parasitism +/− (tick–deer, lives ON host) · Predation +/− (kills) · Competition −/−: INTRA = same sp. (gulls/nest rock), INTER = diff. sp. (lion–tiger) · Keystone = huge effect, removal → collapse (Atlas NP wolves ≠ bees; otters)

**ENERGY** Producer = autotroph (1) → 1° (2, herbivore) → 2° (3) → 3° (4) · heterotroph = eats others · omnivore = 2+ levels (bear) · Arrows: food → eater · 10% up (÷10); 90% → metabolism → HEAT · 3.5M → 350k → 35k → 3.5k kcal · Decomposers (fungi, bacteria, enzymes) → nutrients → soil → producers · scavenger = vulture

**POPULATIONS** Limiting factor = stops growth · DD (worse when crowded): competition, predation, disease, waste, space, "5+ beans die" · DI (same at any size): weather, cold front, flood, fire, hurricane, drought · J (exponential) → overshoot → decline → recovery → ~K (S) · waves around K = cyclical/periodic; predator peaks lag prey · ↑resources → ↑K; habitat loss → ↓K · bear: 100 fish ÷ 10 = 10 bears · bean K ≈ 50
</div>

<div class="idx-card" markdown="1">
<div class="idx-title">BACK</div>

**SUCCESSION** Primary = NO soil (lava, Yosemite granite): lichen/moss 0–150 → grasses 150–500 → shrubs 500–1,000 → climax 1,000+ · Secondary = SOIL stays (fire, farm): grasses 1–20 → shrubs/small trees 20–150 → climax 150+, faster · Tertiary (Hero) = soil lost + humans restore · Climax = stable, final

**FIRE** Surface = floor, canopy OK, big trees live, low–mod severity, clears fuel · Crown = treetops, tree to tree, kills trees, high severity, ladder fuels · Severity = damage; intensity = heat · CZU: Aug 16 2020 dry lightning; wind + slope; >97% Big Basin (oldest CA park, 1902); redwood thick bark + resprouts · prescribed burns cut fuel

**FRAGMENTATION** big habitat → small isolated patches · more edge, less interior · same amount ≠ same habitat (checkerboard worst) · corridors/crossings help

**BIODIVERSITY** Richness = # species · Evenness = equal counts? · Rel. abund. = 1 sp. ÷ total (125/200 = 0.625) · A = B rich (5), B more even; C richest (10) · more diversity → more resilient

**ECOSYSTEM** community + abiotic · equilibrium = balance in normal ranges · biotic incl. dead logs/leaves; abiotic = sun, water, rocks, temp, weather

**GRAPHS** DRY = Dependent/Responding → Y · MIX = Manipulated/Independent → X · time → x · line = over time; bar = categories · correlation ≠ cause

**CER** Claim = 1-sentence answer · Evidence = data + UNITS · Reasoning = science concept (WHY)
</div>
