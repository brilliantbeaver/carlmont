| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | 25 multiple-choice questions · 30 minutes · on your school Chromebook (charge it, bring the charger) |

| **What Ms. Hero wants** | For every topic: know the **definition**, its **importance**, and be able to **identify it in context** (in a scenario, table, or graph). |
| **Absent?** | Contact Ms. Hero right away to make it up. |

## How to use this guide {#home-how}

1. **Read** a topic in the **Study Guide** tab. Each topic opens with a green **"Know it cold"** box, then has diagrams, tables, **"From your handouts"** boxes (blue), **traps** (red), and **model answers** (purple).
2. **Practice** that topic right away with the button at the end of each topic, or in the **Practice** tab. Stuck? Click **Hint**. Wrong on the first try? You'll see *why* that choice is wrong and get one more try. Then you'll get the full explanation and a link back to the exact study-guide section.
3. **Check your Results** — the mastery table shows which topics need more work, and you can **retry only the questions you missed**.
4. **Take the Mock Test** (25 questions, 30-minute timer, no hints) once you've finished the drills — just like the real thing.
5. **Make your index card** using the **Index Card** tab as a model. Writing it by hand is studying!
